THE
PROBLEM.

Home Credit serves underbanked borrowers with thin credit files. The business problem: score each applicant for default risk using alternative data — bureau, prior applications, installments, POS, credit cards.
01
Why it matters
Default rate is low (~8%) — imbalanced classification; naive accuracy is misleading.
02
Cost asymmetry
Missing a defaulter is far more expensive than rejecting a good payer.
03
Calibrated PD
Calibrated PD feeds downstream credit policy, expected loss, and loan pricing.

05 · FEATURE ENGINEERING
2026-10-18

Feature Engineering Pipeline

Starting from 122 raw columns, we engineer 301 features across 4 functional blocks.

01 · APPLICATION CLEANING

  • DAYS_EMPLOYED sentinel (365243) → NaN + flag
  • CODE_GENDER XNA → NaN
  • OWN_CAR_AGE = NaN if no car
  • Time: AGE_YEARS, EMPLOYED_YEARS, REGISTERED_YEARS
  • Ratios: CREDIT_INCOME, ANNUITY_INCOME, CREDIT_TERM, CREDIT_GOODS, INCOME_PER_FAM
  • EXT_SOURCE aggregates: MEAN / MIN / MAX / STD / PROD / COUNT
  • NUM_DOCS_SUBMITTED

02 · BUREAU AGGREGATION

  • Group by SK_ID_CURR
  • BUR_ACTIVE_CNT, BUR_OVERDUE_CNT
  • BUR_CREDIT_SUM, BUR_DEBT_SUM, BUR_OVERDUE_AMT
  • BUR_DEBT_RATIO = debt / credit
  • BUR_LOAN_CNT, BUR_DAYS_CREDIT_MIN
  • BUR_TYPE_* one-hot (per credit type)
  • HAS_BUREAU flag

03 · PREVIOUS APPS + INSTALLMENTS

  • PREV_AMT_SUM/MEAN, PREV_CREDIT_MAX
  • PREV_DAYS_DEC_MIN, PREV_CNT_PAYMENT_MEAN
  • PREV_APPROVE_RATE
  • INST_PAY_RATIO, INST_LATE_MEAN
  • INST_LATE_7D_SUM

04 · POS_CASH + CREDIT CARD

  • POS_UTIL_MEAN, POS_CNT
  • CC_UTIL_MEAN
  • Per-customer aggregations, merged to master
  • Final 301-dim feature vector per customer

06 · METHODOLOGY
2026-10-18

Methodology · Modeling Pipeline

We compare 4 models: 2 logistic-regression baselines (unweighted / class-balanced) and 2 gradient-boosting trees (raw + isotonic-calibrated). Optuna (15 trials) tunes LightGBM hyperparameters; early stopping on the validation fold.
STEPOPERATIONDETAIL
1SplitTrain / Valid / Test (70 / 15 / 15) stratified on TARGET, fixed seed
2PreprocessColumnTransformer: median impute (num) + most-freq (cat) + one-hot (cat, drop-first) + standardize (num)
3BaselineLogistic Regression (LR) + LR-balanced (class_weight='balanced')
4Tuned modelLightGBM (Optuna 15 trials) — n_estimators=205, num_leaves=53, lr=0.047
5CalibrationIsotonic regression on validation — Brier pre=0.0677 → post=0.0678
6ThresholdF1-driven (recall ≥ 80%) — τ* = 0.05
7EvaluateAUC, Gini, KS, Brier, PR-AUC, F1 — test set, 7,500 unseen customers

12 · LIMITATIONS & FUTURE WORK
2026-10-18

Limitations & Future Work

Honest assessment of what this pipeline does and does not yet cover.

LIMITATIONS

  • Sample size — notebook executed on a 12k-row subsample to fit local compute. Re-running on the full 307k rows is expected to lift AUC further.
  • Macro recall vs precision — at τ=0.05 we prioritise catching defaulters; a higher τ would improve precision at the cost of recall. Policy choice, not a model flaw.
  • Drift monitoring — Home Credit portfolio evolves yearly; PSI / KS drift on EXT_SOURCE_*, AMT_* should be tracked quarterly.
  • Fairness audit — disaggregated metrics (CODE_GENDER, AGE_BAND) required before production rollout.

FUTURE WORK

  • Rejected-inference — current training data only includes approved loans; extending to the inference population would close the selection-bias gap.
  • Deep tabular models — TabNet, FT-Transformer, and other deep-learning tabular architectures are next steps.
  • Time-series of installment history — sequence models (LSTM, Transformer) on payment deltas can extract additional signal.
  • Calibration refresh — quarterly re-fit of isotonic regression as portfolio mix shifts.
